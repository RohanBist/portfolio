During my internship at Shangrila Informatic, I got my first real taste of Flutter. Here's what actually helped me get up to speed.

## Why Flutter clicked for me

Flutter's widget-based architecture makes a lot of sense once you stop thinking in terms of HTML and start thinking in terms of composable UI pieces. Everything is a widget — and that consistency is actually a strength.

## The things nobody tells you

State management is where most beginners get lost. I started with `setState` for simple cases and that was the right call — don't jump to Provider or Riverpod until you actually need it.

## What I'd tell myself starting out

Read the Flutter docs properly. They're genuinely good. And build something small end-to-end before trying to understand everything.
